New World RP Rules

To ensure quality roleplay for everyone, please follow these guidelines

Community Standards

  • • Respect all community members and staff
  • • No discrimination or hate speech of any kind
  • • Keep OOC (Out Of Character) drama out of the server
  • • No cheating, exploiting, or hacking
  • • Help new players and foster a welcoming environment
  • • Follow all Discord and in-game rules

Roleplay Guidelines

  • • Stay in character at all times during roleplay
  • • Value your character's life - no unrealistic actions
  • • No random acts of violence (RDM/VDM)
  • • Proper roleplay must precede any criminal actions
  • • New Life Rule applies after character death
  • • No metagaming or powergaming
